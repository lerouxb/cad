The solid, quietly

There is no model-mode toolbar. The model is a drawing on the page, the timeline is the structure, and the selected feature's parameters sit in the timeline detail line.

What this study tests

  • No stage title or status block. The header identifies the file, the timeline identifies the step, and one footer line holds body health.
  • The selected feature is the context. Its distance, direction, operation, and profile live in one line under the timeline; the active step name above is the identity.
  • The name is the handle. “Gusset 45°” is how you find this step again when a downstream reference fails; the detail line offers rename.
  • The stage is the model at the selected step. Steps 06–07 are paused, so the holes are not built here.
  • The highlighter follows the feature. Selecting Extrude 05 washes the gusset, not the whole body.
  • Export is one quiet action. It is the end of the loop, not a separate mode with its own chrome.
  • Measurements are typographic. Overall dimensions sit on the drawing like annotations, not in a stats panel.

Open questions

  • Should the feature detail line expand into a full inline editor, or stay a compact sentence?
  • Is a single overall dimension enough, or should selected faces show their own measurements?
  • Where do bodies and materials live if there is no persistent browser?
“No permanent toolbar” does not mean no affordances. It means every affordance appears next to the thing it changes.
