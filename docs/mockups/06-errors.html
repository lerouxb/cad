When a reference dies

A broken reference is an ordinary state, not an exception. The failed step stays in the timeline, the last good body stays on the page, and the recovery is written in the same voice as the rest of the app.

What this study tests

  • Failure is visible in history. Step 06 is dotted-underlined and marked; step 07 is paused, not deleted.
  • The last good body remains. You can still look at the model while deciding what to fix.
  • The lost reference is named. “Extrude 02 · front” is a human-readable topological name, not an index.
  • Steps are named too. Step 06 is “M4 holes”; the error names the step and the lost face, so you know exactly what to repair.
  • Recovery is three sentences. Reselect, open the sketch, or revert the edit.
  • No red. The error uses ink, a dotted rule, and a single “!”. The highlighter is reserved for the active choice.

Open questions

  • Should a failed step automatically open its diagnostics, or stay quiet until selected?
  • How do we show “using cached frame” on the model without adding another badge?
  • Is a visible “roll back to here” affordance needed on every step?
Never silently drop geometry. The page keeps the last good body and tells you which sentence is broken.
