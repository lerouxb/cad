The draft leaves the sketch

Extrude and revolve are solid operations. The sketch is their input, not their workspace. This study opens the draft on the model, keeps the source sketch on its plane, and makes profiles and the axis pickable in the same 3D view as the preview.

The move

The last frame of the sketch and the first frame of the draft are the same geometry. Only the camera and the preview layer change. The form stays open and the stage stays live.

1 · finish the sketch

The profile is selected in the sketch. extrude… is a feature chip, not a command that applies anything.

2 · the camera tilts

The same plane becomes an oblique sheet in the model. The preview grows through the worker while the camera settles.

3 · decide in 3D

The preview is solid, the sketch is a read-only overlay, and the form's inputs stay live. Orbit, change the value, add a profile, pick the axis.

The live draft

This is the stage the form opens onto. Switch the scene, turn the camera with look at sketch, pick profiles or the revolve axis, and change the value. The same scene is rendered from a normal camera and from the drafting view.

The form is the only commit surface. A background click does not cancel the draft; cancel, Escape and a timeline click do. See DRAFT_VIEW.md §8.

Why the model view

The form was never the problem. The stage was. The model view answers the questions a sketch cannot.

Depth and direction

The preview shows which side of the plane the solid grows from, how far it goes, and whether a cut or join is touching the body. The distance arrow is on the model; the exact value is still the field.

The body in context

The sketch plane may be a face or a datum, and other bodies may be in the way. The model view shows the target body, the plane's frame and the operation's added/removed volume in the shared boolean language.

The input stays in place

Profiles and the axis are sketch geometry on a plane. In the model they are still exactly there, but now they can be picked without leaving the preview. No stage switch, no second selection model.

Picking without leaving the model

Profiles and the axis are two pick modes on the same stage. The form's row and the model tool row show the active family; the preview ghosts while a pick is active.

Profiles

pick makes every region fill a target: material regions and void profiles. A click toggles, Shift adds, box and lasso take a set. The list is the truth. An empty pick keeps the draft; zero profiles disables the primary.

The axis

Candidates are the sketch lines and the X/Y references, highlighted in 3D. The chosen axis is an infinite dash-dot line. A profile that crosses it is visible in cut red, not silently retargeted.

One scene, two cameras

The sketch stage is a camera normal to the plane, not a separate world. look at sketch is a view preset; it does not leave the draft. The long-run direction is an orbitable sketch view: the geometry stays on its plane, but the camera does not have to.

Normal to the plane

The precise authoring view. The same sketch, the same plane, the same preview underneath. For drawing and snapping, this remains orthographic.

Oblique drafting view

The same plane tilted into the model. The body and the preview are in frame; the profile, axis and values are still editable. The camera is the only thing that moved.

Entry and exit

The draft always knows where it came from. Apply keeps the model; cancel restores the origin, including the seed profile selection.

Decisions

  • A sketch-consuming feature opens on the model. The sketch is the input, not the workspace.
  • One scene, two cameras. The sketch stage is a normal-to-plane camera; the draft is the same scene from an oblique camera.
  • The sketch is read-only input. Geometry and regions stay; editing, dimensions and constraints do not.
  • Profiles are a model pick mode. The preview ghosts, the fills are targets, the list is the truth.
  • The axis is picked in 3D but stays in the sketch plane. No silent default; a crossing axis is visible.
  • The form does not change shape. Same popover, same rows, same footer; only the stage behind it changes.
  • The sketch stays on its plane. The camera may orbit; the geometry may not.
  • Cancel restores the origin. Apply keeps the model and selects the new step.

Open questions

  • Should the draft show dimensions and constraint marks over the model, or keep them for sketching only?
  • Should unselected regions be visible outside profile mode, or only when picking?
  • Can a revolve axis be a model edge outside the sketch plane? That needs datum axes.
  • Should look at sketch live in the foot (view-only) or in the form? The study puts it in the foot.
  • Can a draft offer edit sketch, or must the user cancel first?
  • How soon can the sketch stage itself orbit, with drawing still constrained to the plane?
design notes · feature drafts

The draft leaves the sketch

Extrude and revolve are solid operations. Keeping the draft in the sketch stage hides the only thing the user is deciding: the solid in context. This study moves the draft to the model and keeps the sketch on its plane as a read-only input layer.

What this study tests

  • The transition is a camera move. The source sketch and the preview are the same scene; the sketch stage is a normal camera, not a separate world.
  • The form is unchanged. The viewport is the new part; the popover, rows, list, `MeasureField`, operation row and footer stay exactly as they are.
  • Profiles stay pickable. A model pick mode ghosts the preview, makes region fills the targets and keeps the list in sync. No stage switch.
  • The axis stays pickable. Candidate sketch lines and X/Y are highlighted in 3D; the chosen axis is an infinite dash-dot line; a crossing axis is visible, not silently retargeted.
  • `look at sketch` is a view preset. It returns to the normal view without leaving the draft. This is the seed of an orbitable sketch stage later.

What is stylised

  • The 3D geometry is an isometric drawing, not a B-rep. The live scene is for interaction decisions.
  • The extrude scene's preview sits on top of the base, so a cut/intersect choice is shown as a visible overlap error rather than a faked pocket.
  • Box/lasso region selection and the boolean diff meshes are described by the contract; the live study implements click picks and a style change. The distance arrow is draggable; the angle arc is a readout, with the field as the exact input.

Open questions

  • Do dimensions belong in the draft overlay? The proposal is no; they are authoring annotations.
  • When the sketch stage can orbit, does it keep the normal camera as the drawing default?
  • Does a 3D edge axis arrive with datum axes, or can the revolve axis pick accept a model edge earlier?
The contract is DRAFT_VIEW.md. The live stage is a design target; nothing in src/ is imported by this page.
