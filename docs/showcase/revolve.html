Revolve

Sweep a closed sketch profile around an axis in the sketch plane to make the circular forms an extrude cannot: cylinders, tubes, cones, spheres and tori, full or partial. It is a history step like extrude, with the profile and axis as its inputs.

What it makes

The profile is the cross-section; the axis is an infinite line in the sketch plane. A profile touching the axis closes the seam; one offset from it leaves a hole.

Tube

Rectangle offset from the axis. 360° leaves a through hole.

Cylinder

The profile touches the axis; the seam closes into a solid.

Cone

A right triangle with one leg on the axis sweeps a cone.

Sphere

A half-disc with its diameter on the axis sweeps a sphere.

Torus

A circle on one side of the axis leaves a hole in the middle.

Partial · 90°

The angle is 1–360°; the flip chooses the sweep side. A partial revolve has two end faces.

The axis

The axis is a line in the sketch plane: a sketch or construction line, or the sketch X/Y reference. There is no automatic axis. The profile stays on one side or touches it.

Two profiles, one axis

No silent default: without a picked axis the primary is disabled with “pick an axis”.

When it does not work

A profile crossing the axis, a missing axis, an angle outside 1–360°, a self-intersection or an invalid result is a visible error. The last good preview stays; no fallback.

The rules in one place

  • Select first, revolve second. `revolve…` is a feature chip in the profile popover, beside `extrude…`; no click starts a revolve.
  • Axis in the sketch plane. A sketch/construction line or the X/Y reference. No automatic default; the primary is disabled until picked.
  • One side or touching. A profile crossing the axis is an error; a profile on the axis closes the seam; an offset profile leaves a hole.
  • Angle 1–360°. 360° is closed and has no direction; a partial angle gets a flip and two end faces.
  • Same form grammar as extrude. Sketch, profile, operation, MeasureField, footer; the axis row and angle are the additions. The operation row is BOOLEANS.md.
  • One history step. `+ body` for new body, `→ body` for join/cut/intersect; re-editable, suppressible, downstream rebuild.
  • Lost axis is lost support. A deleted axis line is surfaced with reselect/revert; never silently retargeted to X/Y.
  • No silent fallback. Missing axis, crossing profile, invalid body or no base for join/cut/intersect stays a visible error.
  • Kernel exists. `brepjs.revolve` takes a planar face, axis and pivot; this is a UI/history feature, not a kernel spike.

design notes · revolve

A profile, turned

Revolve is the circular counterpart to extrude: one closed profile, an axis in the sketch plane, and an angle. It is a history step, not a direct edit, and it is the natural way to make cylinders, tubes, cones, spheres and tori from the same 2D sketches.

What this study settles

  • Profile → revolve chip. The feature lives in the profile popover beside extrude; a raw click never starts it.
  • Axis is in-plane and explicit. A sketch/construction line or X/Y reference; no automatic default; lost axis is a lost support.
  • One side or touching. The profile may touch the axis (cylinder/cone/sphere) but not cross it; an offset profile leaves a hole (tube/torus).
  • Angle 1–360°. 360° is closed; a partial angle flips and gets end caps.
  • Profile and axis are inputs. The profile row is a list with remove; the axis is a single row + pick, not a wall of chips. See PICKS.md.
  • History step. New body/join/cut/intersect, re-editable, suppressible, with the same face-role and downstream rules as extrude.
  • The operation row is shared. BOOLEANS.md wins: cut shows removed material in red; intersect keeps the overlap and ghosts the rest.
  • Kernel is available. `brepjs.revolve` takes a planar face and an axis; the missing work is the feature, form and worker path.

Where the draft lives

The revolve draft should open on the model, with the profile on its plane and the candidate axes in 3D. The axis is still an in-plane sketch line, but the sweep, the side and the end faces are visible while picking. DRAFT_VIEW.md is the proposed stage; draft-view.html shows the live axis pick.
