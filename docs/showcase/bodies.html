design notes · bodies

Bodies live with their step

Still one timeline — but a body appears next to the step that created it, not in a separate browser. That is the fix for Fusion's split: there, the object list is disjointed from the timeline, so it is unclear that the bodies are the things as they exist at that step. Here the origin is explicit, and rolling back visibly pauses the steps — and therefore the bodies — that no longer exist.

What this study tests

  • One step can make several bodies. One sketch, two disjoint profiles, one extrude → + body, + lid. Bodies that share an edge fuse; disjoint ones stay separate.
  • Later steps reference bodies, not create them. The boss joins → body; the hole cuts → lid. The chip stays at the creating step.
  • Rollback pauses outputs. Select step 2 and the boss and hole disappear with their steps; the bodies line dims on the paused steps.
  • The body popover. Click a chip: appearance, visibility and isolate attach to the body, not the step. This is where the deferred visibility work lands.
  • Identity breaks visibly. “Connect the profiles” merges the two rectangles into one body, so the cut that targeted lid loses its body — ink, a dotted rule and a “!”, with reselect/open sketch, exactly like a lost face.

Open questions

  • Do the steps show only bodies, or sketches and datum planes too?
  • Default target for a new feature: all bodies (Fusion), or the body you last touched?
  • Is the body's name editable in the chip or only in the popover? Do names renumber when a feature is suppressed?
  • When a body is consumed (joined), does its chip strike through at the consuming step, or vanish?
  • The timeline gets long with many bodies — grouping (already a Keep item) is the mitigation, but what does a collapsed group show of its bodies?
Components are deliberately out of scope: a component is a namespaced set of bodies with its own timeline, which is a separate model. The only thing to protect now is the seam — a body id should be namespacable later (componentId/bodyId).
