Section analysis

Section analysis is the one inspect tool that is used constantly: slide a plane through the body and look at the cut, to check wall thickness and internals. It is a view, not a modelling operation, so it must never enter history or the document.

What this study tests

  • The cut is hatched. The classic drafting convention: solid material reads as a hatched cross-section, so the wall is unambiguous.
  • The plane is a handle, not a dialog. Drag it (or use the range) to move the cut; flip keeps the other half.
  • It is view-only. The header says so, and nothing here touches the timeline.

Open questions

  • Is the plane a world plane, a selected face, or a free plane? This study uses a world X plane for clarity.
  • Does it belong with the visibility toggles (isolate, opacity) in one inspect surface? Likely yes.
  • Should the hatched section be exportable, or is it purely on-screen?
This is stylised geometry for a design decision. The interaction is the point: a plane, a position, a flip, and a hatched cut.
